1. Introduction

Hiloox Private Limited ("Hiloox", "we", "our", or "us") provides AI-powered business communication solutions, including AI voice agents, virtual numbers, and WhatsApp Business automation. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when businesses use our platform.

By accessing or using Hiloox's services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • AI Voice Calling Services
  • WhatsApp Business Automation
  • Virtual Number Services
  • APIs and Integrations
  • Web Dashboard
  • Mobile and Desktop Applications
  • Customer Support Services

This policy does not apply to third-party websites or services integrated with Hiloox.

3. Information We Collect

Business Information. We may collect:

  • Company name
  • Business address
  • GST or tax information (where applicable)
  • Authorized representative details
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing information

Account Information.

  • Login credentials
  • Authentication data
  • API keys
  • User roles and permissions

Communication Data. Depending on the services enabled, we may process:

  • Call metadata
  • Voice recordings (when enabled)
  • AI-generated transcripts
  • Call summaries
  • Contact information uploaded by customers
  • WhatsApp message content
  • Message delivery status
  • Virtual number usage logs

Technical Information. We automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • Session logs
  • Error logs
  • Usage analytics

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide communication services
  • Route and connect voice calls
  • Process WhatsApp Business messages
  • Generate AI responses
  • Produce call summaries
  • Improve AI models and service quality (unless contractually excluded)
  • Detect fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Provide customer support
  • Process billing and payments
  • Monitor platform performance

5. AI Processing

Our platform uses Artificial Intelligence to:

  • Answer customer calls
  • Generate responses
  • Convert speech to text
  • Summarize conversations
  • Extract structured information
  • Automate workflows

Customers are responsible for ensuring that they have obtained all necessary permissions and legal bases required to use AI-powered communications in their jurisdiction.

6. Call Recording and Transcription

Call recording and transcription are optional features that may be enabled by customers.

Customers are solely responsible for:

  • Providing legally required notices
  • Obtaining consent where required
  • Complying with applicable recording laws

Hiloox acts as a technology service provider and does not determine the purpose or legality of customer communications.

7. Compliance with Indian Telecom Regulations

Where applicable, Hiloox operates in accordance with relevant Indian laws and regulatory requirements, including applicable directions issued by telecom authorities.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that their use of Hiloox complies with:

  • Telecom regulations applicable to their business
  • Customer consent requirements
  • Do Not Disturb (DND) obligations
  • Anti-spam requirements
  • Applicable licensing requirements
  • Caller identification requirements

Hiloox reserves the right to suspend or terminate services used in violation of applicable telecom regulations.

8. WhatsApp Services

Customers using WhatsApp Business integrations must comply with:

  • WhatsApp Business Platform policies
  • Meta Business policies
  • Customer opt-in requirements
  • Message template approval requirements
  • Applicable anti-spam laws

Hiloox is not responsible for account restrictions imposed by Meta due to customer violations.

9. Data Sharing

We may share information with:

  • Telecom providers
  • Cloud infrastructure providers
  • AI service providers
  • Payment processors
  • Government authorities where legally required
  • Law enforcement agencies upon valid legal request

We do not sell personal information.

10. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed in India or other jurisdictions where our infrastructure or service providers operate. Appropriate contractual and technical safeguards are implemented where required.

11. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards including:

  • Encryption in transit
  • Encryption at rest where applicable
  • Access controls
  • Authentication mechanisms
  • Activity logging
  • Infrastructure monitoring
  • Backup procedures

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services
  • Meet contractual obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Customers may request deletion where legally permissible.

13. Customer Responsibilities

Customers must ensure:

  • They have authority to upload customer data.
  • They obtain all required consents.
  • Communications are lawful.
  • Contact lists are legally acquired.
  • Applicable telecom and privacy laws are followed.

14. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, customers may request:

  • Access to information
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of eligible data
  • Export of available data
  • Restriction of processing where applicable

15. Cookies

Hiloox may use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, analytics, and platform functionality.

16. Children's Privacy

Hiloox services are intended exclusively for businesses and are not directed toward individuals under the age of 18.

17. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of our services after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
